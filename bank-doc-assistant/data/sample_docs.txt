Bank Marketing Dataset - Field Documentation
=============================================

occupation: Type of job or occupation of the client (e.g., administrative_staff, technician, services)
age: Age of the client in years
education_level: Level of education attained by the client (e.g., high_school, university_degree, professional_course)
marital_status: Marital status of the client (single, married, divorced)
communication_channel: Type of communication channel used to contact the client (cellular, telephone, unidentified)
call_month: Month of last contact during the campaign
call_day: Day of the week of the last contact
call_duration: Duration of the last contact in seconds. Note: this attribute highly affects the output target
call_frequency: Number of contacts performed during this campaign for this client
previous_campaign_outcome: Outcome of the previous marketing campaign (successful, unsuccessful, unidentified)
